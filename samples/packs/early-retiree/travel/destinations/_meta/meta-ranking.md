# Destination meta-ranking — the whole corpus at a glance

> A bird's-eye **characteristics profile** of the persona's destination library: what each place
> *offers* across six qualities, scored 1–5. This is the diverge view across the library; the
> per-trip compare radar is the narrowed shortlist. FICTIONAL sample data.

Nine places from Scottsdale for a calendar that is wide open; this household weights Ease and Water, and reach barely matters. The axes here: Food · Nature · Culture · Water · Walkable · Ease.

## How to read it

- **More is more of that quality, not "better."** A 5 on Nature and a 2 on Walkable describes a
  place, it doesn't grade it. Weight the axes per trip: a beach week weights Water and Ease; a
  city stop weights Food and Culture.
- **Reach is effort, not a demerit.** A long flight is a bigger trip, not a worse destination.
  The column exists so the pipeline can sort by how much of the trip is travel.
- **The library grows as trips do.** Every destination doc the persona researches gets a row; the
  matrix is regenerated from those docs, never hand-edited.
