# Print card — ORCL Q4 FY2026

*FICTIONAL sample print card for the demo-investor persona. Not advice.*

## Why this print matters more than usual
This is the legacy concentrated position the household is diversifying down on a schedule. A great
print is the awkward case: it argues for holding while the plan argues for trimming.

## The bar
- GREAT: backlog growth well ahead of revenue growth, cloud infrastructure accelerating again
- GOOD: backlog up, cloud infrastructure steady
- OK: in-line
- BAD: backlog flat or a cloud deceleration

## The read
Backlog jumped on cloud-infrastructure bookings; cloud infrastructure revenue accelerated for a third
straight quarter. **Grade: GREAT.** Reaction +9.4%.

## What changes
The plan's standing rule handles this: trims continue on the calendar, but the *pace* slows while the
story is this strong (the June ticket stages the standard tranche at a slower cadence). Concentration still
comes down; it just comes down a little slower.
